Session lifecycle

Conceptual states derived from run_session and Actor::admitted. END applies while awaiting admission or admitted: shutdown, stale/replaced generation, idle timeout, non-increasing seq, slow consumer, zone loss or client disconnect. Admission refusal is specific to the waiting state. Client close/read failure is ClientGone and sends no server close code.

The bucket permits 30 frames/s, burst 30; exhaustion rejects the frame, not the connection. Rejections can themselves fill the outbound queue and lead to 4429. All inbound frames, including ping/pong, reset the 60-second idle timer. The same checks run before zone admission; rate limiting is drawn once.

Ticket generations are per account; the live SessionRegistry is keyed by player EntityId. It cancels the older socket when a newer generation takes over that entity. Stale commands and late despawns are fenced by generation. Reconnect requires a newly issued ticket.
