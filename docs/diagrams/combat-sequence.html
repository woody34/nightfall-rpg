Combat sequence

01–04: The session supplies the actor; no intent carries damage, HP or rolls. SetTarget checks life, AOI and attackability; Attack needs a live target in AOI and is idempotent (a repeat Acks without a swing). Each tick runs commands, then the chase (out of reach: head for the target), movement, due impacts, then new swings. A swing starts in reach once ready_at has passed; impact and next swing are whole ticks from attack_timing (Squire's Sword at DEX 30: +6 and +12).

05–08: Every tick's AppliedTick (commands, dispositions, all zone events including off-AOI facts and hate, and the end-of-tick state digest) is acknowledged by the log before any frame is released. Each player's stream is responses, AOI diff, then the combat facts it may see in event order; StatsChanged and TargetChanged are owner-only.

09–12: At impact the zone rechecks attacker, target life (incarnation) and reach; a failure is an AttackCancelled with no draw. Otherwise it draws the hit roll and, on a hit, crit and spread, applies damage and emits AttackResult, the target player's StatsChanged, and the NPC's hate (F(d*100/(L+7)), 1 for a miss). HP 0 runs the death consequences below. StopAttack, MoveTo or a new target cancel a pending swing at any point.

Source: state_combat.rs · combat.rs · combat_math.rs · gate.rs · world.proto.

Death and respawn

13–15: HP 0 runs the consequences once per life (a later lethal hit finds the victim dead): swing, chase and selection cleared, every attacker's target and hate forgotten, EntityDied. At tick end one internal Progression fact per changed player rides in the same durably acknowledged AppliedTick for checkpoints (E4.2).

16–17: An NPC's template XP goes to one living player: of the blow's attacker and every player whose swing at that life was due the same tick, the most recorded damage, then the lowest id. XpGained (capped at X[86]−1), one LevelUp per level, one StatsChanged. A player victim pays R((X[L+1]−X[L])*loss[L]) and may de-level; StatsChanged carries HP 0, level and XP. All owner-only.

18–22: A dead actor's other intents get DEAD_ACTOR; reconnect keeps the dead state (PlayerLoad.alive, ReplaceSession). Respawn moves it to the zone safe point with max(1, F(maxHP*65/100)) HP, 0 MP, a new incarnation and 6000-tick protection that an accepted Attack ends; a repeat from the living actor is INVALID. No XP refund.

The NPC side (corpse, decay, slot respawn) is the NPC state machine's Dead state: kill hands the victim to npc_died (E3.4).
