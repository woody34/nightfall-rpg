Combat sequence

01–04: Left-click raycasts NPC proxies first (ANightfallPlayerController::OnClickMove). UCombatStateSubsystem::ClickEntity sends SetTarget then Attack once per target (repeat clicks while pending/acked send nothing; a ground click sends StopAttack only if attacking, then MoveTo). UNetClientSubsystem sends the intents over WebSocket with sequential IDs. The session supplies the actor (no intent carries damage, HP, or rolls). In the zone tick, commands run, target life, AOI, and attackability are checked, chase moves into reach, and swings start once ready_at passes (impact and next swing at whole ticks from attack_timing: +6 and +12).

05–09: Every tick's AppliedTick (commands, dispositions, events, and state digest) is acknowledged by the log before any frame is released. The player's stream receives responses, AOI diff, and combat events in order. UNetClientSubsystem::DispatchServerMessage fans out Ack, which UCombatStateSubsystem::ApplyAck handles to transition AttackState from Pending to Active; own-entity movement reconciles the pawn.

10–12: At impact the zone rechecks attacker, target life (incarnation), and reach; a failure cancels the swing with no draw. Otherwise it draws hit, crit, and spread, applies damage, and emits AttackResult, the target player's StatsChanged, and on lethal hit EntityDied. The tick is durably logged before release. UNetClientSubsystem::DispatchServerMessage fans out typed events to UCombatStateSubsystem, which dedupes AttackResult on tick+attacker+target (repeated facts change nothing and show no second number), ignores stale incarnation or generation, treats StatsChanged, XpGained, and TargetChanged as owner-only, and clears the target on EntityDied or despawn. UNightfallHud reads BuildHudModel each frame in NativeTick (updating target frame, own HP/MP/XP, and floating HP bars over proxies) and spawns one floating damage number per AttackResult. Own EntityDied displays the dead overlay with a Respawn button sending Respawn, cleared when EntityRespawned arrives.
