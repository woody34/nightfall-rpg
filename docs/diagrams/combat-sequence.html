Combat sequence

01–04: Left-click raycasts NPC proxies first (ANightfallPlayerController::OnClickMove). UCombatStateSubsystem::ClickEntity sends SetTarget then Attack once per target (repeat clicks while pending/acked send nothing; a ground click sends StopAttack only if attacking, then MoveTo). UNetClientSubsystem sends the intents over WebSocket with sequential IDs. The session supplies the actor (no intent carries damage, HP, or rolls). SetTarget checks life, AOI, and attackability; Attack needs a live target in AOI and is idempotent (a repeat Acks without a swing). Each tick runs commands, then the chase (out of reach: head for the target), movement, due impacts, then new swings. A swing starts in reach once ready_at has passed; impact and next swing are whole ticks from attack_timing (Squire's Sword at DEX 30: +6 and +12).

05–09: Every tick's AppliedTick (commands, dispositions, all zone events including off-AOI facts and hate, and the end-of-tick state digest) is acknowledged by the log before any frame is released. Each player's stream receives responses, AOI diff, then the combat facts it may see in event order; StatsChanged and TargetChanged are owner-only. UNetClientSubsystem::DispatchServerMessage fans out Ack, which UCombatStateSubsystem::ApplyAck handles to transition AttackState from Pending to Active; own-entity movement reconciles the pawn.

10–12: At impact the zone rechecks attacker, target life (incarnation), and reach; a failure is an AttackCancelled with no draw. Otherwise it draws the hit roll and, on a hit, crit and spread, applies damage, and emits AttackResult, the target player's StatsChanged, and the NPC's hate (F(d*100/(L+7)), 1 for a miss). HP 0 emits EntityDied and runs the death consequences below. StopAttack, MoveTo, or a new target cancel a pending swing at any point. The tick is durably logged before release. UNetClientSubsystem::DispatchServerMessage fans out typed events to UCombatStateSubsystem, which dedupes AttackResult on tick+attacker+target (repeated facts change nothing and show no second number), ignores stale incarnation or generation, treats StatsChanged, XpGained, and TargetChanged as owner-only, and clears the target on EntityDied or despawn. UNightfallHud reads BuildHudModel each frame in NativeTick (updating target frame, own HP/MP/XP, and floating HP bars over proxies) and spawns one floating damage number per AttackResult. Own EntityDied displays the dead overlay with a Respawn button sending Respawn, cleared when EntityRespawned arrives.

Death and respawn

13–15: HP 0 runs the consequences once per life (a later lethal hit finds the victim dead): swing, chase and selection cleared, every attacker's target and hate forgotten, EntityDied. At tick end one internal Progression fact per changed player rides in the same durably acknowledged AppliedTick for checkpoints (E4.2).

16–17: An NPC's template XP goes to the living player whose validated attack actually landed the killing blow. Cancelled same-tick swings and historical damage confer no credit. XpGained (capped at X[86]−1), one LevelUp per level, one StatsChanged. A player victim pays R((X[L+1]−X[L])*loss[L]) and may de-level; StatsChanged carries HP 0, level and XP. All owner-only.

18–22: A dead actor's other intents get DEAD_ACTOR; reconnect keeps the dead state (PlayerLoad.alive, ReplaceSession). Respawn moves it to the zone safe point with max(1, F(maxHP*65/100)) HP, 0 MP, a new incarnation and 6000-tick protection that an accepted Attack ends; a repeat from the living actor is INVALID. No XP refund.

The NPC side (corpse, decay, slot respawn) is the NPC state machine's Dead state: kill hands the victim to npc_died (E3.4).
