Nightfall · Process · Minimal light

Run, acknowledge, then release

AppliedTickDraft → ZoneState::run_tick → AppliedTick → DurableTickGate::admit. Every 100 ms, inputs are drafted in receive order, with at most 8 per session; excess waits. The gate appends AppliedTickRecord and awaits the JetStream ack. Only then does the actor broadcast non-idle ticks; sessions encode their player’s ordered outputs and the writer sends them to sockets. Idle ticks are still logged.

Append failure holds the same record: no broadcast, next draft or snapshot. Backoff is 10 ms–1 s; after 5 s the zone pauses and sessions reject intents with OVERLOADED. A successful ack resumes it.

Replay: open_epoch requires a snapshot and completion watermark, and checks contiguous records. ZoneState::from_snapshot restores state; run_tick re-runs recorded drafts. AppliedTickRecord::reproduced_by compares encodings, hashing outputs when the stored record uses SHA256. This is a library/test path, not an automatic restart recovery service.

NF_ZONES: nightfall.zone.<zone>.<epoch>.{snapshot,applied,watermark}. The Postgres zone_snapshots row indexes the epoch-start snapshot. Best-effort NF_SESSIONS audit frames are not replay inputs.
